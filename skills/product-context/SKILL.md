# Product context

Each project keeps one file, `.agents/product-marketing.md`, holding the facts that copy, pages and plans draw on. Gathering them once keeps them consistent. Other skills, including the upstream SEO skills, read this same path.

## What belongs in it

Include only what can't be inferred from the code or the site, and what the user has confirmed:

- **Product.** What it is, in one sentence a customer would say, plus its category.
- **Customer.** Who buys and who uses it, the trigger that makes them start looking, and what they used before.
- **Problem and outcome.** Use the customer's words. Quote real phrasing from reviews, calls or tickets where you have it.
- **Alternatives.** What else they consider, and the honest reason someone picks this product instead.
- **Proof.** Numbers, named customers, testimonials and guarantees, each with its source and date. Copy may only make claims drawn from this list.
- **Objections.** What makes people hesitate, and how each is answered.
- **Voice.** A few adjectives, plus words to use and words to avoid.
- **Commercials.** Pricing, and the primary conversion (trial, demo or purchase).

Record unknowns as open questions rather than filling them with guesses.

## Building it

Draft it first from what's already available, such as the repo, README, site, docs and the conversation. Then ask only about the gaps, a few questions at a time. Keep the file short, because other tasks read it at the start and every line should earn its place. When facts change, update the file in place.
